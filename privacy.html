Privacy Policy

This policy describes what the current static StanKit Studio site processes in your browser, what it saves locally, and when third parties may receive ordinary network information.

Effective and last updated: August 21, 2026

Key privacy, rights and independence summary

    1. Scope and operator information

    This policy applies to the StanKit Studio website at https://stankit.vercel.app/, including its photocard print-layout maker, fan-letter draft builder, and information pages.

    Site operator: Wang Qingsen (王庆森), an independent individual based in Pukou District, Nanjing, Jiangsu, China. StanKit Studio is not represented as a registered company or professional team. Public and confidential contact routes are described in Sections 4 and 10. No affiliation or endorsement by an artist, management company, agency, label or platform is claimed.

    The operator’s full residential unit address is kept private for personal safety. The complete, accurate postal address may be provided directly to payment, identity-verification or legal service providers when required.

    2. Images, drafts and exports processed in your browser

    • Uploaded images: image content selected in the photocard tool is decoded, edited and placed into a layout in the current browser. The application does not intentionally upload or store those images on a StanKit Studio server.
    • Fan-letter drafts: template selection, text replacement and editing take place in the current browser. Drafts may be saved locally only when the tool’s history or favourite features are used, as described below.
    • Exports: PDFs or images you generate are created for download to your device. StanKit Studio does not keep a server-side copy of the exported file.

    Local processing does not mean the page makes no internet requests. The website and some libraries are delivered by third parties, as explained in Section 5.

    3. LocalStorage and session storage

    The tools may use browser storage on the device and browser profile you are using:

    • stankit_lang may store the selected interface language.
    • stankit_photocard_history may store recent print/export settings and summary details. It is designed not to contain uploaded image files.
    • stankit_history and stankit_favs may store fan-letter draft text, recent copied drafts and bookmarked drafts.
    • stankit_analytics_consent stores whether you allowed or declined optional GA4 analytics.
    • stankit_attribution may temporarily store sanitised campaign parameters and a referring host in session storage for the current browser session.

    These items remain in browser storage until the tool removes them, you clear them, or the browser removes them. Clearing cookies and site data for StanKit Studio in your browser will delete this local information. Clearing only download history will not necessarily clear LocalStorage.

    The contact page does not save a name, email address, feedback text or prepared message in LocalStorage or session storage.

    4. Contact form and public GitHub Issues

    The contact form has no message backend. Submitting the form creates a text block in the page, attempts to copy it, and attempts to open the project’s new GitHub Issue page. Nothing is sent to StanKit Studio until you paste the text into GitHub and submit the issue yourself.

    GitHub issues are public and are processed under GitHub’s own terms and privacy practices. Do not include an email address, private message, account credential, identity document, home address or other sensitive personal information.

    For confidential privacy or legal requests, contact the operator at simon602653879@hotmail.com instead of using GitHub. The email address is publicly listed on this site to provide a private communication route. Email delivery and storage are handled by the sender’s and recipient’s email providers.

    5. Hosting and third-party requests

    When your browser requests a site page, library, font, analytics resource or external link, the relevant provider may receive ordinary network and request information such as your IP address, requested URL, date and time, browser/device details, referring page and cookies that the provider is permitted to read.

    • Vercel hosts the website and may process request and security logs.
    • Tailwind CDN and Google Fonts may deliver interface code and fonts on the main tools.
    • cdnjs or jsDelivr may deliver jsPDF code used to create PDFs in the browser.
    • Google Analytics 4 (GA4) may receive analytics requests only when a real GA4 Measurement ID is configured and the visitor allows optional analytics. An empty value does not enable GA4.
    • Ko-fi receives a request when you open a Ko-fi page. These information pages use a normal external link and do not load the former floating Ko-fi widget.
    • GitHub receives a request when its page is opened and receives the issue content only when you submit it there.

    The application is not designed to include uploaded image contents or fan-letter draft text in requests to Tailwind, font, jsPDF, GA4 or Ko-fi providers.

    6. Analytics and advertising status

    GA4 is conditional: its script is not loaded unless a valid, real Measurement ID is configured and the visitor selects “Allow analytics.” If allowed, Google may process page and allow-listed tool-event information along with ordinary network information. The site does not put names, email addresses, letter text or image content into analytics event fields. Visitors can reopen Analytics settings and change their choice.

    At the date of this policy, the audited site source does not load a Google AdSense ad script on these information pages. If advertising is enabled later, this policy must be updated to identify the advertising providers, cookies or other storage used, purposes, choices and consent flow. Where required for visitors in the EEA, United Kingdom or Switzerland, the site owner must configure a Google-certified consent management platform before serving personalised ads.

    7. Cookies and browser controls

    The current tools primarily use LocalStorage and session storage for the functions described above. Third parties may set or read cookies when their services are loaded or opened, subject to their policies and your browser choices.

    You can block third-party requests or storage through browser settings and extensions, although doing so may prevent fonts, PDF creation, analytics, external contact or support links from working. You can remove StanKit Studio’s saved tool data by clearing the site’s browser data.

    8. Data retention and security

    StanKit Studio does not maintain an account database or a server-side database of uploaded images, generated files or contact-form submissions. Local data remains under the retention controls of your browser. Third-party providers apply their own retention and security practices to network logs, analytics, public issues or support-page activity.

    No internet service can promise absolute security. Avoid processing or posting sensitive personal information with these tools.

    9. Children’s privacy

    The tools do not require an account and are not intended to solicit personal information from children. A child should not post personal information to a public GitHub issue. A parent or guardian should supervise use where required by local law.

    10. Questions, corrections and policy changes

    For non-sensitive privacy questions or corrections, use the contact page and submit the prepared text through the public GitHub issue tracker. Do not post identity documents or confidential information there.

    For confidential privacy or legal requests, email simon602653879@hotmail.com. Include only information reasonably necessary for the request and do not send passwords or payment-card details. Material changes to this policy will be reflected by the effective date above.
