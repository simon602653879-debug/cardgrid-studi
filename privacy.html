Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 19, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to StanKit Studio ("we", "our", or "us"), accessible via https://stankit.vercel.app. We are committed to honoring and protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we process, why we process it, and how your privacy rights are safeguarded when using our free web utilities.

2. Our Core Principle: 100% Client-Side Processing

Unlike traditional web applications that upload your files to remote servers for processing, StanKit Studio operates entirely in your browser (client-side):

  • Photos & Images: When you upload images to the Photocard Print Maker, your images are rendered locally onto an HTML5 Canvas / jsPDF engine. They are never transmitted, saved, or stored on our servers.
  • Fan Letters & Custom Text: All fan letter text generation, customized slots, and particle replacements occur directly in JavaScript within your local browser session.

3. Local Storage (LocalStorage)

We use your browser's local storage feature (localStorage) solely to enhance your user experience:

  • stankit_lang: Remembers your selected interface language preference.
  • stankit_letter_history: Allows you to view and retrieve your recently drafted fan letters on your current device.
  • stankit_photocard_history: Saves recent print layouts locally so you can easily reprint without re-uploading.

This data is stored purely on your local machine and is never sent to our servers. You can clear this data anytime via the in-app "Clear History" buttons or by clearing your browser cache.

4. Third-Party Advertising & Google AdSense

We may display advertisements provided by Google AdSense and other third-party ad networks to support the operational costs of keeping our utilities free:

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to users based on their visit to our sites and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings or via aboutads.info.

5. Web Analytics (Google Analytics 4)

We use Google Analytics (GA4) to understand aggregate usage trends, such as page views, popular tools, and navigation events (e.g., scenario selection count, PDF downloads). All IP addresses are automatically anonymized by GA4, and no personally identifiable information (PII) is tracked.

6. Third-Party Links & Services

Our website contains voluntary links to external platforms such as Ko-fi (for optional donations) and GitHub. We encourage you to review the privacy policies of any third-party websites you visit.

7. Children's Privacy

StanKit Studio does not knowingly collect any personal information from children under the age of 13. Since all our file manipulation occurs strictly in the local browser without user registration, children's personal information is neither requested nor stored.
