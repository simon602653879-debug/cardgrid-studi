📚 Photocard Printing FAQ & DIY Guide

Learn paper selection, sizing standards, and home printing recommendations

📏 What is the standard size for idol photocards?

Standard idol photocards measure exactly 55×85mm (2.16×3.35 inches) with rounded corners (r=2.5mm~3mm). Instax Mini polaroids measure 54×86mm. StanKit Studio supports both dimensions with pixel-perfect millimeter precision.

📄 Which paper should I use for printing at home?

We recommend 250gsm~300gsm double-sided glossy or matte photo paper. If your home inkjet printer supports it, 300gsm RC glossy paper provides the most authentic official merchandise feel and thickness.

🔒 Are my uploaded photos safe and private?

100% yes. StanKit Studio processes and arranges all images purely inside your web browser using HTML5 Canvas & Web Workers. Zero images or user data are ever uploaded to external servers.

🖱️ How do I reorder or delete cards on the grid?

Simply drag any card with your mouse and drop it onto another slot to swap positions. To remove a photo, hover over its card slot and click the red ✕ delete button.
