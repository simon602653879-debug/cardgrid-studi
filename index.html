🛒 4×6" Retail Photo Paper (2×2 Grid) Printing & Budget Guide

Based on standard 4×6" photo prints available at major pharmacies and retail kiosks (e.g. Walgreens, Walmart, CVS, typically $0.15~$0.25/sheet depending on local promotions and regional store pricing). By selecting our 4×6" (46×70mm 2×2) template, you can arrange 4 photocards onto a single sheet to maximize paper efficiency.

  • Select 4×6" Photo Paper format in StanKit Studio and export PDF/JPEG.
  • In the retail kiosk app, select "Fill / True-to-size" (disable auto-crop).
  • Use a craft paper cutter or scissors along our dashed scissor cut-lines.

🛡️ Photocard Archival & Acid-Free Storage Rules

Never use cheap PVC binder pages! PVC contains chemical plasticizers that migrate into photocard ink over time, causing irreversible yellowing, sticking, and holographic foil peeling.

  • Always choose Acid-Free & PVC-Free (PP or CPP) card sleeves.
  • Double-sleeve valuable photocards (56×87mm inner snug sleeve + 58×89mm outer hard sleeve).
  • Store binders vertically in a cool, dry place away from direct UV sunlight.
