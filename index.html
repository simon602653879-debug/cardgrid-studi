Choose your desired paper format (A4, Letter, 4x6, 5x7). Upload high-resolution images of your favorite idols, anime characters, or memories.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the standard official K-Pop photocard size?

Official album photocards (BTS, Stray Kids, Seventeen, TWICE, NewJeans, Xiao Zhan) are standardly 55 mm × 85 mm (2.16 × 3.34 inches) with rounded corners. This tool formats your prints precisely to 55×85mm.

Are my uploaded images saved on your servers?

No. All cropping, rendering, and PDF generation happen directly inside your web browser using HTML5 Canvas. Your photos never leave your device.

What paper should I use for printing?

We recommend using 230gsm to 300gsm heavy glossy inkjet photo paper or double-sided photo paper for the most authentic album photocard feel.
