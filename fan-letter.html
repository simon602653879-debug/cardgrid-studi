📚 Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ) & Cultural Guide

Learn essential honorific rules, Weverse commenting tips, and cultural etiquette for global idol messaging

❓ What is a K-Pop Fan Letter and why do Korean honorifics matter?

In Korean culture, respecting honorifics (존댓말) is paramount. Using informal language (반말) improperly can sound rude or intrusive. StanKit Studio automatically modulates grammatical endings (해요체 / 합쇼체) according to your selected relationship and scenario to ensure your letter is polite, respectful, and touching.

⚡ How to write a Weverse or Bubble message that idols actually reply to?

Keep your messages concise (under 30 characters), visually appealing with high-recognition kaomojis, and ask warm, relatable questions (such as asking what they had for dinner or wishing them a good sleep). Avoid long essays in live comment feeds as they easily get scrolled past.
