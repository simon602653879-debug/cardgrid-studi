About this project

Small browser tools for fan-made projects

StanKit Studio provides a photocard print-layout maker and an editable fan-letter draft builder. It is maintained by Wang Qingsen (王庆森), an independent individual operator based in Nanjing, Jiangsu, China, with no account required for these tools.

Last updated: August 21, 2026

Unofficial and independent

StanKit Studio is an unofficial fan-made utility. It is not affiliated with, operated by, sponsored by or endorsed by any artist, management company, agency, label or platform. Names and other references remain the property of their respective rights holders.

What the tools do

Photocard print layouts

Arrange images on supported paper sizes and export a print-ready file. Uploaded image content is processed in the current browser rather than sent to a StanKit Studio image-processing server.

Open the print tool

Editable fan-letter drafts

Start from prewritten templates and customise the result. The builder is a template tool, not a promise of AI translation, cultural review or guaranteed honorific accuracy.

Open the fan-letter tool

Product principles

Clear scope

Describe what the current static tools actually do without inventing user counts, certifications or professional-team claims.

Local processing

Keep uploaded image content in the browser while clearly disclosing browser storage and third-party network requests.

User control

Let users review drafts and exports, clear saved site data, and understand their responsibility for third-party content.
